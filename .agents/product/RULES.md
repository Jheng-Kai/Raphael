# Raphael product rules

Public paraphrases of decisions dated 2026-10-20 through 2026-10-25. Later rules take precedence. Each row is one requirement. Explicit conflicts remain `human` with both versions. `hard` violations fail judge regardless of score; `human` needs product/native acceptance. This ledger records requirements, not claims of compliance. Mechanical references use `repository/path::exact test title`.

Owner follow-up (2026-10-30), R-UI-45 supersedes the week strip and painted hover:
four equal Home blocks keep the same full/sparse/empty layout. Hover lights only the
border with var(--hover-edge); background, transparent click layer and inner content
do not change, including on press. Keep whole-block navigation and keyboard focus.
Home has a 128px emblem (96px at 720px) and the original --fs-h1 report, still horizontal at 720px, followed
by an adaptive calendar: compact mini month and selected-day agenda side by side
at 1120/1280x800, stacked at 720; a full event-chip month fills the remaining area
only with sufficient container width and height. Both keep six rows, dimmed
adjacent dates, today and event marks. Empty/full data keep the same layout.
A fixed month/year header, previous/next icons and Today stay in place. Arrow
buttons, horizontal trackpad and touch swipes change month with
a short slide. In compact mode dates select a day in place; full-grid dates and event chips open
Calendar on that day/event. Blank block space opens the selected Calendar day.
The visible Coming up label is removed. home-hover-content and home-month enforce
these contracts in the shared rendered workflow.

# Raphael product rules: conversation and members

## A member starts its own sub-workers

A member could only hand work to another member (`handoff`, `ask_member`), one task at a time for each member; the owner wanted a member to split a job into parts that run side by side, with any model or CLI they connected, as Claude Code's sub-agents do. A sub-worker is a task of the member (the one runner, `background.service`), never a second runner, and what it may do is what its member may do.

## A failure is information: a member keeps going until no plausible way is left

Members gave up too fast. A member treats a failed call as information, takes the next plausible way, and asks the owner only for what only the owner can give, or when the ways left cost far more than the result is worth. Cause, from a language-teaching member's turn (told to use its own computer for a news page, it read the lead, had its page refused twice, ended by declining to work around the block and never opened its browser): every word of the harness on that path said stop. These rows change the harness for every member and every brain; no member's own routines, skills, instructions or memory are edited.
